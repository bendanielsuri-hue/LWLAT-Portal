Preset reasons are the sentences offered in a dropdown wherever the panel asks why something happened. They are suggestions, not a closed list — whoever fills the form in can always choose "Other" and write their own, and what they choose is stored as the sentence itself. Rewording or deactivating a preset here changes what the next person is offered; it never changes a reason somebody has already given.
